Product thesis by Kinshuk Attri
SagriVision should become the trust layer between farm action and enterprise climate claims.
Sagri already has a strong technical wedge: AI field boundaries, satellite intelligence, digital soil mapping, process-based models and carbon traceability. The product opportunity is to orchestrate these capabilities into a decision and assurance system that makes evidence quality, scientific limits and allocation logic visible throughout the programme lifecycle.
Map-first onboardingEvidence graphScenario-to-programmeReproducible MRVNo double countingAudit collaboration
01 · Core problem
Climate teams cannot confidently connect corporate targets to what happened on each farm.
Data is fragmented across growers, suppliers, maps, models, procurement and consultants. Scientific outputs arrive too late and are hard to defend.
02 · Product wedge
Sagri can reduce both measurement cost and coordination cost.
Satellite and AI reduce spatial and verification effort; a governed workflow reduces repeated reconciliation, evidence chasing and audit preparation.
03 · Differentiation
Do not sell a calculator. Sell an auditable operating system for agricultural decarbonisation.
The durable advantage is the traceable chain from field geometry and activity evidence to model outcome and enterprise allocation.

Product principles
Rules that prevent a polished UI from weakening scientific credibility
Evidence before outcome
Every number can be traversed backwards to inputs, source, reviewer and version.
Measured, modelled and allocated are never conflated
Users always know whether a value came from direct data, inference, modelling or allocation.
Uncertainty is a product object
Confidence, model domain and conservative scenarios are visible in planning and reporting, not buried in an appendix.
One field, many workflows
A reusable field evidence graph supports Scope 3, regenerative programmes and credit methodologies without duplicate data collection.
North-star metric
Auditable hectares with outcome allocated to a verified physical flow
This combines programme coverage, evidence quality, scientific completion and enterprise utility. It is harder to game than enrolled hectares or calculated tonnes alone.
